# Imagens da seção "Leituras selecionadas" (landing)

Quatro prompts de **fotografia realista** para gerar no **Magnific com Nano Banana**, um por card. Eles seguem `docs/linha-visual-imagens.md`: a mesma luz de janela, o mesmo pós e os mesmos materiais das fotos da seção Proposta, com o assunto tirado da referência de Hermes Trismegisto (o sábio ancião contra o escuro, o códice, a esfera armilar), sem coroa de estrelas, planetas, brilho nem glifos.

## Como gerar

- **Proporção:** 2:3 vertical, 2k. A imagem fica embaixo do texto de cada card, numa placa quase quadrada, e desliza com a rolagem e com o mouse. Por isso cada quadro tem detalhe de cima a baixo, com o assunto no centro.
- **Ordem:** gere primeiro o **I**. Depois use essa imagem como **referência** no Magnific para gerar o II e o III, para que o ancião (rosto, barba, manto) e a luz saiam iguais. No IV, use uma das fotos da Proposta como referência de luz e cor.
- **Negativo:** se a tela do Magnific tiver campo de prompt negativo, cole o bloco correspondente.
- **Salvar:** exporte em WebP para `public/images/leituras/` (nesta pasta da landing) com os nomes `01-hermetismo.webp`, `02-poimandres.webp`, `03-sete-esferas.webp` e `04-asclepio.webp`.

## I · O que é o hermetismo (`01-hermetismo.webp`)

**Por que esta cena:** é o ancião da referência, de pé e de três quartos contra o escuro, segurando o códice fechado junto ao peito: o hermetismo como um conjunto de escritos guardado e atribuído. Colunas, estantes e piso dão detalhe de cima a baixo.

```
CAMERA: IMAX MK IV 65mm at ISO 250, locked off at hip height in a library aisle, tilted five degrees up.
LENS: Hasselblad/Zeiss 80mm T2.2 at T4, five metres away, focus on the codex and hands.
LIGHT: One soft window key at 4.300K from high frame-left, zero fill. Beard and codex at 65 IRE, face at 50 IRE, robe at 25 IRE, stone and shelves at 15 IRE, midtones held, no flat black where there is detail.
SUBJECT: An elderly man with a long white beard and bare head stands full length in three-quarter view, turned toward frame-left, eyes lowered, face visible but small. Both hands press a closed brown leather codex with worn brass clasps to his chest.
FOREGROUND: Worn stone floor slabs, a low stack of bound volumes at the lower right edge.
MIDGROUND: The man sharp, beard strands, leather grain and clasp wear readable.
BACKGROUND: Two pale stone columns flank him; walnut shelves of bound volumes rise to the top edge, faint but readable.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain oxblood wool robe in heavy matte folds, no ornament or trim, hem covering the feet; undyed linen at the collar.
POST BEHAVIOR: 65mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, deep blacks, warm muted midtones, low saturation.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Figure on the vertical centre line, head in the upper third, codex at centre, hem in the lower fifth; shelf, stone and floor detail in every band, no empty strips.
```

## II · Poimandres: a visão de Hermes (`02-poimandres.webp`)

**Por que esta cena:** segundo o tratado, a visão vem quando os sentidos do narrador ficam contidos, como num sono pesado. O ancião de olhos fechados, com as mãos quietas sobre o manuscrito, mostra só esse recolhimento; a parede grafite quase vazia sugere a vastidão sem nada que brilhe.

```
CAMERA: IMAX MK IV 65mm at ISO 250, locked off at seated chest height, tilted slightly down across a reading table.
LENS: Hasselblad/Zeiss 80mm T2.2 at T4, focus on the hands and manuscript, shelves softening.
LIGHT: One soft window key at 4.300K from high frame-left, zero fill. Brow and beard at 60 IRE, pages at 65 IRE, robe held at 25 IRE, wall at 8 IRE.
SUBJECT: An elderly man with a long white beard sits at a dark oak table, eyes closed, head tilted slightly forward, as in heavy sleep. Both hands rest open on a large open manuscript of yellowed pages, writing faint and illegible. Waist-up framing, face small in frame, real weathered skin.
FOREGROUND: Worn front edge of the oak table with one thin highlight, robe folds spilling over it.
MIDGROUND: Man and manuscript sharp, beard strands, paper fibre and wool weave readable.
BACKGROUND: Deep graphite wall, nearly empty, the edge of a tall bookcase in shadow above his shoulder.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain oxblood wool robe, matte, heavy folds holding soft gradation, no embroidery, no shine.
POST BEHAVIOR: 65mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, deep blacks, preserved warm muted midtones, low saturation.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Figure on the vertical center axis, crown and shoulders in the top fifth under shelf shadow, manuscript in the middle band, table edge and robe folds filling the bottom fifth.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
```

## III · As sete esferas (`03-sete-esferas.webp`)

**Por que esta cena:** segundo o Poimandres, sete governadores envolvem o mundo em círculos. A mão do ancião sobre uma esfera armilar de latão com sete anéis lisos traz isso como instrumento de estudo, sem planetas nem céu. Mão em cima, esfera no meio e pedestal embaixo preenchem as três faixas.

```

LENS: Zeiss Makro-Planar 65mm T2.2 at T4, focus on the brass rings, fingertips sharp, shelves softening.
LIGHT: One soft window key at 4.300K from high frame-left, zero fill. Brass at 60 IRE with small speculars, hand at 45 IRE, table at 18 IRE, shadows graphite with readable midtones.
SUBJECT: The weathered hand of an old man descends from the top of frame, two fingertips resting on the upper ring of an antique brass armillary sphere. Exactly seven plain smooth concentric rings, no markings, each tilted at a different angle, circle a small matte dark iron ball at the center that does not shine. The sphere sits on a turned walnut pedestal.
FOREGROUND: Front edge of a worn walnut table, grain catching a thin highlight.
MIDGROUND: Rings and hand sharp, brass patina, knuckle creases readable.
BACKGROUND: Dark wooden bookshelves with leather spines sinking into shadow.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain oxblood wool sleeve, matte weave, no shine.
POST BEHAVIOR: 65mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, deep blacks, warm muted midtones, low saturation.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Vertical axis on the center line: hand and sleeve in the top third, sphere in the middle third, pedestal and table in the bottom third, texture in every band.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
```

## IV · O Asclépio em latim (`04-asclepio.webp`)

**Por que esta cena:** o Asclépio sobreviveu sobretudo numa tradução latina copiada de manuscrito em manuscrito. O copista transcrevendo a partir de um códice mais antigo mostra essa transmissão, sem nenhum símbolo religioso. Estante em cima, copista e páginas no meio, cadernos embaixo.

```
CAMERA: IMAX MK IV 65mm at ISO 250, locked off at chest height, three-quarter view from front-right, tilted down onto a slanted copying desk.
LENS: Hasselblad/Zeiss 80mm T2.2 at T4, focus on the quill tip and ruled leaf, shelves softening.
LIGHT: One soft window key at 4.300K from high frame-left, off frame, zero fill. Parchment at 65 IRE, hands and face at 45 IRE, wool at 20 IRE, graphite shadows, midtones held.
SUBJECT: A middle-aged copyist in half body, full short hair, head bowed, face small and half in shadow, writes with a goose quill on a ruled parchment leaf. Beside it an older Latin codex lies open, dense faded illegible lines, no illumination. A horn inkwell and a penknife on the ledge.
FOREGROUND: Under the desk, stacked unbound quires with frayed edges and cord ties.
MIDGROUND: Hands, quill and both pages sharp, the copyist on the vertical centre line.
BACKGROUND: Above his head, a walnut shelf of bound quires in brown and oxblood leather, plain spines.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain oxblood wool habit, matte coarse weave lit on shoulder and sleeve, folds sinking to graphite.
POST BEHAVIOR: 65mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, deep blacks, warm muted midtones, low saturation.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Vertical stack on the central axis: shelf in the top fifth, head and hands mid-frame, desk and quires filling the lower third, no empty band.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
```

## Checklist das quatro juntas

- [ ] São fotos, não pinturas nem renders.
- [ ] Mesma luz (janela do alto à esquerda), mesmo grão, mesma cor das fotos da Proposta.
- [ ] O ancião do I, II e III parece a mesma pessoa.
- [ ] Bordô só em lã e couro; latão só em pequenos brilhos; nada de azul ou roxo.
- [ ] Nada proibido pela marca (lista em `docs/linha-visual-imagens.md`).
- [ ] Detalhe no topo, no meio e na base de cada imagem.
